How I Built an SEO-Friendly School Website for B.R. International School

This project focused on building a school website that is easy for parents to understand and easy for search engines to crawl.

Visit B.R. International School Back to Blog

Project goal

The goal was to present B.R. International School with clear pages, simple navigation, fast-loading visuals, and content sections that support admissions, trust, and school discovery.

SEO approach

The page structure uses descriptive headings, direct school-focused wording, optimized metadata, useful internal sections, and clean links so visitors can move from homepage content to important school information quickly.

Result

The finished website gives the school a stronger online presence with a responsive layout, gallery support, search-friendly structure, and a direct live website link from the portfolio.
