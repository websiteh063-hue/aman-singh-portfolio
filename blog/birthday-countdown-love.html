Overview

Birthday Countdown Love is a live website deployed on Vercel for countdown-based celebration pages. The project is added to the portfolio as a simple, mobile-friendly web experience focused on birthdays, love-themed presentation, and event anticipation. A countdown website works best when visitors can understand the emotion and the remaining time quickly, especially on mobile.

This article gives the project a dedicated SEO page with a canonical URL, BlogPosting schema, sitemap entry, internal links, and a direct backlink to the live Vercel deployment. The homepage card gives quick access, while this page explains why the project belongs in the portfolio.

Project Purpose and User Experience

The purpose of Birthday Countdown Love is to make a special date feel visible and shareable. Countdown pages are often opened from phones, so the project needs fast loading, readable text, and simple navigation. The portfolio description focuses on birthday countdowns, love-themed celebration UI, and mobile-friendly event anticipation.

The live project link is included in the homepage card, blog topic card, article hero, article body, sidebar, and SEO link hub. That gives visitors several clean paths to inspect the website without making the page confusing.
